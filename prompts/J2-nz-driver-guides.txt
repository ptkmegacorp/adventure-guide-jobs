Find New Zealand adventure tour companies that hire DRIVER-GUIDES or TRIP LEADERS for multiday tours (3+ days), similar to Haka Tours, Flying Kiwi, and Stray Travel.

## Must match
- NZ-based or NZ-departure tours
- Driver drives the vehicle AND guides the trip (or dedicated trip leader on multiday loops)
- Small-group adventure (not large coach mass tourism)

## Sources — USE
workabout.co.nz, company websites, careers pages, tourism industry job listings

## DO NOT USE
indeed.com, linkedin.com/jobs, ziprecruiter.com, glassdoor.com, or any generic job aggregator. Do not visit or cite them.

## Search hints
- "driver guide" OR "tour leader" New Zealand adventure -site:indeed.com
- workabout adventure guide
- {company} careers guide

## Output
Use whatever format is natural and compact: bullets, short sections, or a table are all fine. For each useful employer, include company, role title, careers URL, trip types, season, hiring signal, fit, and license requirements when visible.

Aim for useful NZ employers; stop when you have enough solid notes rather than forcing a fixed count.
