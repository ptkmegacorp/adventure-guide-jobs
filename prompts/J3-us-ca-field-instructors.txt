Find USA and Canada employers hiring FIELD INSTRUCTORS, PROGRAM LEADERS, or TRIP LEADERS for multiday experiential travel programs (2 weeks to one semester), similar to Pacific Discovery, Where There Be Dragons, ARCC Programs, Rustic Pathways, and Broadreach.

## Regions
United States and Canada only.

## Must match
- Multiday programs with overnight travel
- Lead/facilitate a student or young-adult group (gap year, summer, study abroad OK)
- Outdoor/adventure/service/cultural immersion focus

## DO NOT USE
indeed.com, linkedin.com/jobs, ziprecruiter.com, glassdoor.com, monster.com. Skip all generic aggregators.

## Sources — USE
coolworks.com, backdoorjobs.com, seasonaljobs.com, company careers pages, "work with us" on program operator sites

## Output
Use whatever format is natural and compact: bullets, short sections, or a table are all fine. For each useful employer, include company, country, role title, careers URL, program length, audience, hiring signal, and fit.

Aim for useful employers; stop when you have enough solid notes rather than forcing a fixed count. Flag active 2025–2026 summer or fall hiring pages when visible.
