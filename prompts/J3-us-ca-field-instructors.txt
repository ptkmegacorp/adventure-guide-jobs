Investigate this SPECIFIC candidate set of USA/Canada-adjacent multiday group travel / experiential program employers for on-trip GROUP LEADER hiring signals. The core target is multiday group travel leadership, not only rugged outdoor/adventure instruction. Look for field instructor, program leader, trip leader, tour leader, trip manager, travel director, destination host, or guide roles. Do not do broad discovery first; use search only to find a missing careers/work-with-us page for a listed candidate.

## Regions
United States and Canada only.

## Must match
- Multiday programs with overnight travel
- Lead/facilitate a student or young-adult group (gap year, summer, study abroad OK)
- Outdoor/adventure/service/cultural immersion focus preferred, but broader multiday educational or small-group travel leadership is also relevant

## DO NOT USE
indeed.com, linkedin.com/jobs, ziprecruiter.com, glassdoor.com, monster.com. Skip all generic aggregators.

Aim for 5–8 useful candidates from the list. If evidence is incomplete but the company clearly runs multiday programs, preserve it as a watchlist candidate rather than discarding it. Flag active 2025–2026 summer or fall hiring pages when visible.
