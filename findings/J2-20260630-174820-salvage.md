 Our Expert Local Guides 
 
 We believe it’s the passion, energy and expertise of our local guides that truly sets Haka Private Tours apart. Sourced from across the country, each guide has their own take on what makes New Zealand so special.

 They will take care of the driving and all on-the-road logistics for you, and provide insightful commentary on the way Our guides are not only fantastic and safe drivers, but are also certified in First Aid and are trained in how to respond in an emergency.

 They all genuinely love to share with you their local knowledge. Whether they’re an expert in hiking, culture, wildlife, snow or adventure activities, you’re guaranteed to have only the best on tour with you.

 
 
  

 
 
 
 
 
 
 Meet Some of our Guides

 Vaughan 
 Vaughan is a travel industry vetran with over 20 years in the industry! He’s fortunate to have visited over 54 countries and loves meeting new people from different places and sharing stories, especially his passion for all things Kiwiana – the All Blacks included! 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Rhys 
 Rhys grew up in the mighty Waikato and was heavily involved with Scouts from a young age. In 2014, Rhys studied Outdoor Education which is where his love for the NZ and the outdoors really took off. For the following six years, he worked winters at Mt Hutt Ski Field and the summers working in outdoor education. Rhys guided for Haka Tours for three season before starting his residential building apprenticeship, but is now back guiding Haka guests around our beautiful country of Aotearoa. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Howie 
 Howie was born and raised in England and “fell in love” with the great outdoors at age 17. He has guided and led trips across NZ and also led twenty high school expeditions to the developing world. He is fond of a good coffee, a good yarn and has a reputation for telling absolutely terrible dad jokes. He loves sharing the best parts of Aotearoa with his guests and is particularly passionate about the natural world.
